Abstract

This record registers, before Gaia DR4 (planned for 2 December 2026), a test of wide binaries. It is one of the checks of Mathematical Materiality (MM): the outcome concerns MM's reading of wide binaries, not the framework. Two MM readings are compared. The main one is a chain of correspondences that takes no parameter from the binaries. It links three things that seem unrelated: The field equation (QUMOND) is borrowed. The declared alternative is the reading of MM 2.54, which says that wide binaries are Newtonian. MOND with the external field is reported as a reference. The data and the rules are fixed now. The pairs of El-Badry, Rix and Heintz (2021) will be re-measured in DR4, with the cuts frozen on 11 October 2026. Stars with a DR4 non-single-star solution are removed. A forward simulation models hidden companions. The decision rule compares the chain with Newton and has written validity conditions: the test can favour one reading, or come out inconclusive or not valid.
