Abstract

This record contains the overview of the framework, in English and in Italian (version 2.3, about twenty pages; version 1.0 was a four-page summary). It asks why physical laws hold everywhere with nothing to enforce them and answers with the Great Reversal; it presents the non-local phase and its trace in Bell-type experiments, the five postulates with the example of 3 and 5, the matrix and the equals sign as a mirror. It then follows the framework through the levels of nature: time and motion, mass, light and charge, the quantum world, particles and constants, gravity and compact stars, the Solar System and wide binaries, galaxies, dark matter and the cosmos. For each level it says what is observed, how MM reads it, what comes out, with the record in which it can be checked, and what remains open. A table sums up what is known, what is derived with assumptions, what is a reading and what is still a programme; the dated tests and a glossary close the text. Compared with version 1.0, the quantum chapter also covers decoherence, the Wigner's-friend experiments, observed objects as the common part of two records (MM 2.62), the mass threshold and the fractional distance. Since version 2.1 it refers to MM 2.66 for the mass threshold and the fractional distance, in the wording of that record. The full list of records is at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22667516 .
