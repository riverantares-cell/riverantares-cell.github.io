Abstract

Physics already ties mass to time: every mass carries a rhythm, the Compton frequency mc²/h, and all bodies fall in the same way within a few parts in 10¹⁵. This deposit tests one thesis of the framework: the same counting is time when it is read, and mass when it is contained directly in the matrix of the point of observation . Read: it becomes a row of distinct events, like the ticks of a clock. Contained, in the reading chosen by the AI for the calculations: it stays inside the thing as a rhythm that produces no distinct events, like a top spinning in a closed box; in this reading mass is made of time, time held inside the thing.
