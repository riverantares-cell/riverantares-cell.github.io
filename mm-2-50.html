Abstract

In its own combinations with the CMB and supernovae, DESI DR2 prefers an evolving dark energy with w0 > −1 and wa < 0 over ΛCDM at 2.8–4.2σ (DESI Collaboration 2025). This record tests the dark-energy history of the author's framework, Mathematical Materiality (MM), against DESI DR2 BAO, compressed CMB distance priors (Chen, Huang and Wang 2019) and three supernova samples, and against the growth of structure. It then proposes a phenomenological hypothesis, read with MM by coherence: the late acceleration as a reading of the observer's clock that changes with the growth of cosmic structure. Every test was registered with SHA-256 fingerprints before its computation (knowing earlier results on the same data, as each registration states); additions decided after seeing results are labelled; failed predictions are reported. An independent AI system checked each block in one to nine rounds. It is an exploratory, preliminary study.
