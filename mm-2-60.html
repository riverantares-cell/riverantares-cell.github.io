Abstract

Cassini ranging data bound the external-field quadrupole of the Solar System at Q₂ = (1.6 ± 1.8)·10⁻²⁷ s⁻² (Park et al. 2026). The wide-binary chain registered in MM 2.56 has these ingredients: Taken down to the Sun with the same field equation, it predicts Q₂ = 3.22·10⁻²⁶ s⁻², 17σ above the measurement (17.0–19.4σ over eight input choices; the MM 2.56 solver gives the same number within 3·10⁻⁵). A local reading of the external field. Here the anomaly lives in the relation between two bodies. What it gives. Registered for Gaia DR4. The reading has its own orbit library, the unchanged forward model of MM 2.56, and a decision rule against Newton (threshold ±5 in log-likelihood, subsets ±2).
