Abstract

A model of the universe developed with the assistance of GPT-6 Astra and Claude has survived its first global confrontation with cosmological data. Across supernovae, DESI measurements and the geometry of the cosmic microwave background, it obtains almost the same fit as the standard cosmological model: a chi-square of 1420.02, compared with 1421.19 for ΛCDM. This is not a statistical victory over ΛCDM. The news is that the construction did not collapse. Starting from a fundamentally different account of cosmic acceleration, Mathematical Materiality 2.32 reached almost the same agreement with three major families of observations.
