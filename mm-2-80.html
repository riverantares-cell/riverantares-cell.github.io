Abstract

The connection between the two sectors. The dynamics of the framework ( MM 2.70.2 ) gives linearised Einstein gravity in the weak field by construction, static strong fields and Friedmann's equations for the homogeneous model; it does not contain a₀. The low-acceleration sector uses the interpolating function of the radial acceleration relation of disk galaxies (McGaugh, Lelli and Schombert 2016), g = g N ·ν(y) with y = g N /a₀ and ν(y) = 1/(1 − e −√y ), where g N is the Newtonian acceleration of the baryons; the framework's choice is a₀ = cH₀/6 (H₀ = 67.4 km s −1 Mpc −1 ), and it reads ν as a sum of reading steps, ν = Σ n≥0 e −n√y . The two sectors are connected by a rule motivated by a premise of the author («the galaxy it sits in affects the observation GCD, which in turn determines its own field»; GCD translates the Italian MCD, which in the framework's vocabulary is a unit of reading, not the arithmetic operation) and completed by stated hypotheses: a system's effective boost g/g N cannot exceed the boost of the context it sits in, min(ν(y), ν(e)), with e the context's Newtonian field in units of a₀. The translations were developed with Claude, an AI assistant; the final construction of the minimum was proposed by the second AI that checked every step.
