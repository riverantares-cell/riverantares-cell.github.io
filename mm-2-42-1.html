Abstract

Version 2.42.1 (correction). The clock law tested here (a0 ∝ 1/t) was a translation introduced by the AI assistant, not a premise of the author, whose own reading of the distal scale is the Hubble radius c/H, i.e. a0 ∝ H(z). Section 5 of this record already found that, with a free amplitude, H describes the MUSE-DARK III bins better than the clock law (χ² 5.1 against 14.9); with the local calibration fixed, the ordering reverses (χ² 16.4 for the clock law and 25.2 for H). The follow-up record «Can one hidden-sector reading cover several dark-matter puzzles?» (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23329080) adopts a0 ∝ H(z) as a phenomenological law chosen from these data and registers it for future data. CT1 stays as registered; every program, result and log is unchanged (new section 12 of the README).
