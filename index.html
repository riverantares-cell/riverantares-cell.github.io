Mathematical Materiality

Ugo Lanciano · Independent researcher, Locri, Italy · ORCID 0009-0008-7618-9635

Why are the laws of physics the same everywhere and at every instant, even though nothing forces matter to obey them? Mathematical Materiality (MM) proposes an answer: the laws are not rules imposed on matter from outside; matter itself is made of mathematical relations, and a relation binds two distinct terms everywhere and always. The works below follow this answer through quantum mechanics, galaxies, dark matter, cosmology, the constants and compact stars.

All works are archived on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0 and were developed with the help of AI assistants, as declared in each record. Each entry below links to an abstract page, the full text in PDF and the Zenodo record.
