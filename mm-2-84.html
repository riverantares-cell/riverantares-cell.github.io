Before we start: the words you need

What an anomaly is. In physics an anomaly is a measurement that does not fit. The measured value differs from what the accepted theory predicts. Sometimes the discrepancy later disappears: it was a measurement error. Sometimes it stays, and then it is a clue that something is missing from the theory.

Standard physics. It is the physics accepted today. It includes the Standard Model of particles and ΛCDM cosmology, that is, cold dark matter plus dark energy. In this text we call it «standard physics», and the abbreviation is FS.

σ, the distance from the data. Every measurement has an error. It is written with the ± sign. The letter σ is read sigma, and it is the typical size of that error.

Saying that a number is at 3σ means one thing: it is three times its error away from the measured value. The larger the number, the less likely the difference comes from chance alone. In this text the zones are these. Below 2σ there is agreement. Between 2σ and 3σ there is tension. From 3σ upwards the reading that gives that number falls.

z and gain. The letter z denotes exactly that distance, in units of σ. It is written z = (datum − model)/σ. In the text there are two z values. One is z_FS: the distance of the datum from standard physics. The other is z_MM: the distance of the datum from the MM reading. The gain is the difference between the two, each taken without sign: |z_FS| − |z_MM|. The gain itself keeps its sign: if it is positive, the MM reading is closer to the datum; if it is negative, it is further away.

What MM is. Mathematical Materiality, MM for short, is the author's framework of ideas. Its proposal is this. Matter is not described by mathematics. Matter is made of mathematical relations.

The words that belong to MM.

  • A premise is a starting sentence of the author. It is a fixed point, like a postulate.
  • A reading is an interpretation, written so that it can be tested. A reading «holds» or «falls». It is the reading that falls, not the whole framework.
  • The GCD, in school mathematics, is the greatest common divisor. In MM it means instead a unit used for counting. Here it enters only through the number δ.
  • Readings are divided into three classes. Class a is a reading already present in the MM material published before. Class b requires a new choice or premise, declared. Class c is a coincidence with no structure, and it does not count.

R and δ. The letter R denotes a ratio between two forces. It takes the strengths of the strong force and the weak force, and carries them to the Planck scale, that is, to a very high energy. There it is 1.0761. It is not an invented number: it is built from the measured values (MM 2.38).

The letter δ is read delta. It is the small relative difference between the two forces: δ = (R − 1)/(R + 1) = 0.036655, that is about 3.7 %.

Registered. It means written, dated and fixed before the calculation. Or before the measurement exists. To fix it a SHA-256 hash is used: a code computed from a file. If the file changes even by a comma, the code changes. That way one can show that a text was already written on that date, and it cannot be changed afterwards.

The two measurements of the neutron lifetime. A neutron outside the nucleus does not last forever: after a while it decays. Its mean lifetime is measured in two ways.

  • The bottle method: the neutrons are closed in a container and those left after a certain time are counted.
  • The beam method: a beam of neutrons is passed through, and the protons born from the decays are counted.

The two methods should give the same number. They do not.

A few more words.

  • H₀ is read H-zero. It is the rate at which the universe is expanding today.
  • a₀ is read a-zero. It is a very small acceleration. Below it the stars of galaxies turn too fast.
  • Cosmic background, or CMB: the light left over from the earliest times of the universe.
  • CKM is a table. It says how quarks mix with one another. The «deficit» is a small shortfall: a sum that should give 1 and instead gives a little less.
  • Status of an anomaly. Open means the discrepancy is at least 3σ and is not explained. Reduced means it has shrunk. Contested means an independent measurement does not confirm it. Closed means it is no longer there.

In short

The census. Physics today has about thirty anomalies. They are measurements that differ, or used to differ, from standard physics by a few σ. Here we have collected twenty-nine of them, each with its primary source. 24 were collected on 1 November 2026, another 5 on 11 November. Today the status is this: 14 open, 8 reduced, 5 contested, 1 in slight tension, 1 closed.

For every anomaly we asked two things. How far the datum is from standard physics, that is z_FS. And how far it is from the MM reading, that is z_MM, when MM has one.

What came out. In twelve cases MM says the same thing as standard physics, or gains nothing net. They are the Hubble constant, lithium, helium, S₈, A_L, curvature, birefringence, the W mass, R(D), the proton radius, X17 and the flyby. In some of these a single datum moves further from MM: S₈ with DES, the W mass with CMS.

In seven cases MM gives a number of its own. The number goes in the direction of the datum, or it sits where standard physics gives none. They are the scale a₀, the neutrino masses, the neutron lifetime, the CKM deficit, the outer rotation curve of the Milky Way, the large angles of the cosmic background, the dark energy of DESI. Of these, the Milky Way is not established, and the large angles give only a weak clue.

In eight cases the comparison is missing, or it is only partial or exploratory. In one MM goes the opposite way: the missing mass in galaxy clusters. And one is closed: the muon g − 2.

The largest difference, in the direction of the data, is the neutron lifetime. Standard physics is 4.4σ from the beam-minus-bottle difference. The MM reading, δ/3, is 0.3σ from it. It is a reading taken from the data, not a prediction.

The registered prediction. To give it a date that others can check, we registered something on 2 November 2026. We wrote what the next beam experiment, BL3 at NIST, must measure: 888.47 ± 0.42 s. Standard physics says about 877.8 s.

The prediction package was sent by certified electronic mail on 3 November 2026. It is contained in this record, with its hash.

1. How the comparison is made

First the data. Every anomaly has four things. The measured value. The value expected in standard physics. The primary source: almost always an arXiv abstract, or a local copy, saved with the hash. And the status: open, reduced, contested or closed. There are exceptions, for example values read from a web page or from a review: these are stated in the notes.

Then the MM reading. It is taken from the already published MM material, and it is class a. Or it comes from a new declared choice or premise, and it is class b. Coincidences with no structure are class c, and they do not count.

The measure of the comparison. We compute z = (datum − model)/σ. When the model has an error of its own, the two errors are added in quadrature; where a row says otherwise, the row holds. Then we take the gain: |z_FS| − |z_MM|.

Here there is a criterion of the author, and it must be said. What counts is the difference from accepted physics, and on which side the datum falls. Who gave the number first does not count.

The Mercury requirement. It is kept separate from the rest. It asks for one of two things. Either a number that comes from a rule published beforehand, with no choice made looking at the datum. Or a reading taken from the data that also reads a second fact, not used to choose it. No row of this record satisfies it yet. The BL3 prediction is made precisely so that it can satisfy it.

Contamination. Whoever wrote the readings knew almost all the measured values. For this reason no comparison has the value of a blind test. Where a form was chosen looking at the datum, we say so.

Checks. The checks were done by Astra (Codex) and Argon (Gemini). Outcomes and limits are written in the individual outcome notes. Some numbers were not checked by a verifier: for example those of 05_dieci_anomalie/NOTA_ESITO_v1.md. This text was checked by Argon, in two rounds, and by Astra, in one round. The reports and the corrections are in the folder registrazioni/.

3. The neutron lifetime (C1) and the CKM deficit (C2)

The datum. The neutron lifetime is measured in two ways. With the beam the protons from the decay are counted: Yue 2013 gives 887.7 ± 1.2 ± 1.9 s. In the bottle the surviving neutrons are counted: UCNτ 2021 gives 877.75 ± 0.36 s. The beam exceeds the bottle by 1.13 ± 0.26 %.

In standard physics there is one single lifetime. So that difference is a problem: the discrepancy is 4.4σ.

The MM reading. It starts from the author's premises, written on 2 November 2026. These are his exact words: «una particelle è un MCD frazionale ed è proprio la frazione che forma lo spazio vuoto fra l'osservatore e l'oggetto osservato» («a particle is a fractional GCD, and it is precisely the fraction that forms the empty space between the observer and the observed object») and «è mcd che frazionato crea spazio e moltiplicato rallenta il tempo» («it is the GCD that, fractioned, creates space and, multiplied, slows time»). They were written after seeing which forms agree with the data.

Now the translation into a number. The translation is ours. It says this: a method that reads the «space» of the fraction sees the lifetime lengthened by δ/3.

The number δ is (R − 1)/(R + 1), with R = 1.0761. That R is the strong/weak ratio at the Planck scale, with three-loop running (MM 2.38). So δ = 0.036655, and δ/3 = 1.222 %.

The same δ can also be read another way: as a slowing, that is as «time». Read like that it gives δ²/2 = 0.00067. This number goes to the first-row CKM unitarity deficit, where the data say 0.0017 ± 0.0007.

What was chosen. The number δ comes from earlier work, 2.38. The form δ/3 was instead chosen looking at the neutron data. There are two bridges, and they are declared. The first: «the neutron observed in two ways reads space». The second: «the 3 are the three quarks that bind». It is therefore a reading from the data, class b. It is not a predicted result.

The second fact: tritium. The tritium lifetime is also measured in two ways. One can count the helium-3 accumulated, as the beam counts the protons. Or one can follow the decline over time, as the bottle does.

Before opening the sources we registered six rules for the divisor, each with its prediction. The registration is of 12 November 2026, at 12:39:42 UTC. Then we looked at 13 measurements, made between 1947 and 2000, in the table of Lucas and Unterweger 2000.

The comparison rests on an added assumption of ours. The author's premises do not contain it. The assumption is this: helium-3 counted in absolute terms «reads the fraction» also in a nucleus. There is another limit too: the chemical state of the samples is not documented, and could shift every z by about one unit.

Under these assumptions, here are the results. Some rules make the «3» hold for every particle, or make it count the nucleons: those are in tension or incompatible in the basic combinations, from −2.8σ to −5.3σ. Then there is the comparison between two forms. On one side the 3 of the quarks: δ/3 for the neutron, δ/9 for tritium. On the other the 4 of the three products plus the parent: δ/4, which is class c. Between these two tritium does not choose stably. It depends on how three old measurements, from 1947, 1955 and 1967, normalised helium-3.

The coincidences. We made an illustrative model. The true effect is taken uniform in the logarithm between 0.1 % and 10 %, with Gaussian error 0.2593 %. Here is what comes out. A single value fixed in advance falls within 1σ of the neutron datum about 1 time in 10. If one chooses among δ/2, δ/3 and δ/4, about 1 time in 4. The calculation is in registrazioni/07_trizio/NOTA_ESITO_v4.md, section 7.

4. The registered prediction for BL3

The complete text is in two places. In registrazioni/02_previsione_BL3/ there is the registered Italian. In PREDICTION_BL3_EN.md there is the English version.

The measurement. BL3 stands for Beam Lifetime 3, and it is an experiment at NIST. It uses a beam of neutrons; the protons are trapped and counted. The goal is an uncertainty below 0.3 s. Data taking is expected from 2027 (F. Wietfeldt, CIPANP 2025). As of 2 November 2026 no BL3 result had been published.

The rule. τ_BL3 = τ_bottle × (1 + δ/3). The rule also says which bottle to use. One takes the bottle measurement of a single collaboration: the one with the smallest total error, published before the BL3 result.

The number, with today's bottle (UCNτ 2021): 888.47 ± 0.42 s. Standard physics gives 877.75 s. The difference is 10.7 s. With the goal of BL3, that is about 20σ.

The zones. If |z_MM| is at most 2, there is agreement. Between 2 and 3 there is tension. From 3 upwards the δ/3 reading of the neutron falls. The datum prefers MM if |z_MM| ≤ 2 and |z_FS| ≥ 3. It prefers standard physics in the opposite case, that is |z_FS| ≤ 2 and |z_MM| ≥ 3. Otherwise it does not decide.

The variant δ/4. This form also exists, for anyone who wants to compare it: it gives 885.79 ± 0.40 s. With an error of 0.3 s, BL3 would separate the two variants at about 5σ. And a result at the bottle value, about 877.7 s, would make the neutron reading fall.

The dates. The rule and the programme were registered on 2 November 2026, at 10:22:05 UTC. There are two corrections, at 10:25:13 and at 12:49:45 UTC, which do not change the number.

The prediction package is made of two files. A zip of 33,268 bytes, with SHA-256 hash 4cc2a83301e28ca2ae95f8402b6cbda9232b20f76d30cd58fecefe71074668dc. And a PDF of 51,963 bytes, with SHA-256 hash 6f8ba827219a184d0b6714f110cde9c5b5975e96f899029af2e3640c2f3d1cee. The package was sent by certified electronic mail on 3 November 2026, with delivery at 08:23:50 UTC. The provider's receipts remain with the author.

The PDF and the list of hashes of every file in the zip are in registrazioni/03_pec_BL3/. The same files are in registrazioni/, and anyone can recompute their hash. The zip is not redistributed, for a precise reason: it also contains the copy of a third party's web page, that is, the BL3 abstract. Its hash is checked on the provider's receipts, which remain with the author.

What an unfavourable outcome hits. It hits the δ/3 reading of the neutron, that is, the premise of 2 November with its two bridges. It does not hit MM as a whole.

5. Other readings tried

Milky Way (B3). Here the covariance of the errors of Jiao 2023 was needed, but it is not published. We reconstructed it from the vector figures of the paper, under declared assumptions.

Then we applied the rule of 2.70, in full QUMOND, with the gas separated from the stellar disc. MM gives a slope of −0.10 between 19.5 and 26.5 kpc. The datum is between −0.38 and −0.42.

In every scenario the MM slope is closer to the datum than the flat comparison curve. That flat curve is registered as a reference, and it is not a ΛCDM prediction. There is, however, a model that does better than MM: an NFW halo of 0.5·10¹² solar masses with the baryons gives −0.14.

The reading is not established. The value z_MM goes from −1.6 to −3.7 over all the scenarios tried. In the scenarios that pass the diagnostic check it is −2.22, −3.02 and −3.68.

There is also a later check, on the APOGEE giants. It shows something important: it is enough to change the distance catalogue, from astroNN to StarHorse, to change the sign of small velocity gradients. The distances of the far stars matter. The files are in registrazioni/04_via_lattea/ and, for the distance check, in registrazioni/08_starhorse/.

Large angles of the cosmic background (A12). We tried three readings of our own. They are suggested by a premise of the author of 20 October 2026, «l'inizio è una diffrazione in comune». They are not, however, consequences of it: aperture, cut-off and window are choices added by us.

Reading L0 is the same as ΛCDM. Reading L1 puts in a finite aperture, which takes power away from the largest scales. Reading L2 puts in an axis given by our motion.

L1 lowers the quadrupole by a third, from 1021 to 676 μK²; the datum is 226. It improves the quadrupole and S₁/₂ by about half a σ. Here a limit must be stated: S₁/₂ is computed from the spectrum as for a full sky, and the effect on the statistic with the mask is not assessed. It is exactly there, with the mask, that the Planck anomaly sits. So it is a weak clue. And the cut-off of the large scales had already been proposed by others: Contaldi 2003, Melia and López-Corredoira 2018.

L2 does not satisfy the registered geometric criterion. With the central Planck directions, the hemispherical asymmetry points 80-86° away from our motion, against a limit of 45°; the QML direction has an uncertainty of ± 31°.

There is then a prediction of L1 for polarisation, registered on 15 November 2026. It changes appreciably only multipoles 2 and 3: EE goes down by 25 % and by 5 %, TE by 63 % and by 17 %. The problem is that there the cosmic variance of our single sky is larger than the effect. We simulated 20,000 skies for each case, with the registered criterion: the measurement always stays in the «does not decide» zone. So polarisation at large angles alone has a very low power to decide L1. The files are in registrazioni/05_dieci_anomalie/A12/.

Neutrino masses (A6). MM gives normal ordering, masses of 0.36 / 8.7 / 50.3 meV and a sum Σ = 0.0593 eV. The sum is 0.6 meV above the minimum from oscillations. The number comes from 2.38; the relation and the number had already been given by Brannen in 2006. Today it holds. It does not, however, resolve the tension between cosmology and oscillations.

Early JWST galaxies (B6). Here there is a reading of ours, which starts from a premise of the author: «un universo letto di traverso».

The reference is a model with constant efficiency. Against that reference we tried a «jump» family, refitted to each data series. The threshold falls between z ≈ 10 and 11; with Sun-Yan it falls between 9 and 10. In the adopted error model, the jump family shows no significant discrepancy. The regular laws tried, instead, are in tension or fall.

One point, however, is not verified: there is no single fixed jump common to all the series. The jump tuned on the Donnan data alone overestimates McLeod at z ≈ 13.5. So the reading is plausible, not verified. ΛCDM reads the same behaviour as an efficiency that changes, and the samples differ by factors of 2-4. The files are in registrazioni/09_jwst/.

Clusters (B2). With the galaxy rule about half the mass is missing. There is a reading taken from the data, under verification. It is not part of this record.

6. What this work does not claim

  • It does not claim that MM explains the anomalies. In twelve rows MM coincides with standard physics or gains nothing. In eight the comparison is missing or partial. In one it goes the opposite way.
  • It does not claim that the neutron lifetime is «derived». The number δ comes from earlier work. The form δ/3 was chosen looking at the datum.
  • It does not claim that tritium confirms δ/3. Tritium does not choose between δ/3 and δ/4.
  • It does not use the zones as automatic exclusions of a theory. They are operational criteria of the project.
  • The meanings of σ are those of the individual tests, with their assumptions. And the rows are not independent of one another.

7. What may decide, or reduce the uncertainty

  1. BL3, with data taking expected from 2027. The number is 888.47 s with δ/3, and 885.79 s with δ/4. If the beam coincides with the bottle, it will be about 877.7 s.
  2. Gaia DR4, announced for December 2026. It will give two things. The wide binaries (B4), with a procedure already published (MM 2.70-2.80). And better distances for the outer stars of the Milky Way (B3).
  3. The three old papers on tritium: Novick 1947, W. M. Jones 1955, P. M. S. Jones 1967. One must see how they normalise helium-3. They could already choose between δ/3 and δ/4.

The REGISTRO.txt files contain the UTC time and the SHA-256 hash of every file at the moment of registration. The folder registrazioni/ also contains the outcome notes, the corrections, the Python scripts and the logs. The author's premises are in the files PREMESSA_UGO_*.md, with the exact words.

The file REDAZIONI.md lists eight copies in which personal data were replaced: mail addresses, links to private chats, a surname of the author written by mistake. For those files the hash differs from the local register.

How to redo the calculations. The scripts run with Python 3 and numpy. The downloaded sources are indicated by arXiv identifier or DOI, in the notes.

A note on the registered files. The working files in registrazioni/ are the registered originals, in Italian. Where the English README and the registered files differ, the registered files hold.

Glossary

  • a₀: the acceleration below which the stars of galaxies turn faster than the visible matter would allow.
  • Anomaly: a measurement that differs from what the accepted theory predicts.
  • Beam method: the protons born from the decays of a beam of neutrons are counted. Bottle method: the neutrons are closed in a container and those left are counted.
  • CKM: the table that says how quarks mix. The «deficit» is a small shortfall in a sum that should give 1.
  • Class a / b / c: a reading already present in the MM material / one that requires a new choice or premise / a coincidence with no structure.
  • Cosmic background (CMB): the light left over from the earliest times of the universe.
  • δ: the number (R − 1)/(R + 1) with R = 1.0761. It is the small relative difference between the strong and weak forces at the Planck scale (MM 2.38).
  • GCD (greatest common divisor): the common counting unit of MM. Here it enters only through δ.
  • H₀: the expansion rate of the universe today.
  • Mercury requirement: a number that comes from a rule published beforehand, with no choice made looking at the datum. Or a reading taken from the data that also reads a second fact, not used to choose it.
  • MM, Mathematical Materiality: the author's framework. The earlier records are cited by number: 2.27 presentation, 2.38 constants, 2.50 dark energy and growth, 2.58 chain of H₀ and a₀, 2.70 dynamics.
  • Premise: a starting sentence of the author, a fixed point like a postulate.
  • R: the strong/weak ratio at the Planck scale, that is 1.0761 (MM 2.38).
  • Registered: written, dated and fixed with a hash before the calculation, or before the measurement exists.
  • SHA-256 hash: a code computed from a file. If the file changes even by a comma, the code changes.
  • σ (sigma): the typical size of the error of a measurement. The distance of a number from the datum is counted in units of σ, and it is z. Below 2 there is agreement; between 2 and 3 tension; from 3 upwards the reading falls.
  • Standard physics (FS): the physics accepted today, that is, the Standard Model of particles and ΛCDM cosmology.
  • Status of an anomaly: open (a discrepancy of at least 3σ, not explained), reduced, contested (an independent measurement does not confirm it), closed.
  • z_FS, z_MM: the distance of the datum from standard physics and from the MM reading, in units of σ. The gain is |z_FS| − |z_MM|, and it keeps its sign.
